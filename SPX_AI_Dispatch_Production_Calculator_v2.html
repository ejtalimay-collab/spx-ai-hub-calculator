How the calculator works

Estimated Volume per Area = Total Volume × Area % Share

Projected Rider Headcount = Estimated Volume per Area ÷ Target Productivity, rounded up to the next whole rider.

Estimated Dispatch Productivity based on Actual HC = Estimated Volume per Area ÷ Actual Rider Headcount.
